## Maps to

Regular Week 10 — idea generation (Criterion 3, first 45 min) + Paper 1 project-specific mock: all 7 parts applied to the student's own project (second 45 min). SKILLS previews both in a single 45-min session.

## Preview

SCAMPER / morphological ideation for C3; then the Paper 1 7-part format (a = describe, b = draw, c = three ideas, d = develop, e = final, f = plan, g = evaluate) mapped to the students' own project rather than a generic product.

## Deepen

Advanced ideation — morphological analysis, biomimicry; what makes C3 a distinction (range, genuine difference, annotation quality). The key insight of the project mock: parts (c), (e) and (f) produce real folder evidence *and* exam practice in the same sitting — the mock is not extra work, it *is* the project.

## Taste beyond

Pugh matrix for concept selection; professional design communication — how a design agency pitches three concepts.

## TA prep

Help peers in both halves of the regular session. Ideation phase: break fixation; common error — restyling one idea three times; cue: *"What's a completely different way to carry the load?"* Paper 1 mock phase: navigate the question; common error — not reading the command verb; cue: *"What verb does part (c) use — and does your answer do that verb?"*

## Challenge

Complete your C3 idea generation to distinction standard *and* draft responses to all seven Paper 1 parts for your own project. Keep both — the mock responses are folder evidence.

## Run sheet

0–10 preview (ideation + P1 structure); 10–20 advanced ideation; 20–35 Paper 1 project-mapped practice; 35–45 TA cue + challenge.

## Take-away

Bring your distinction-level C3 and your Paper 1 draft responses to Term 2.
