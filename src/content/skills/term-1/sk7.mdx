## Maps to

Regular Week 9 — 3D crating applied to the project form (deepening W2) + design annotation: annotating sketched ideas against the specification. The combination that separates top-band Paper 1 answers.

## Preview

Crating applied to the actual project form; the SPEC-linked annotation model — every annotation traces to a spec point or a design decision.

## Deepen

Combining view with annotation on one page — layout discipline; annotating form (shape choices), function (how it works) and material (what it is and why) simultaneously; the "so that …" extension: "timber rails — so that the load is carried in the grain direction."; the three-part annotation: *feature + reason + trade-off*.

## Taste beyond

Presentation drawing and render in professional practice; how industrial designers communicate design intent on one page; annotation in patent drawings and technical documentation.

## TA prep

Help peers write annotations with depth; common error — arrows pointing to a feature with just its name ("timber frame"), not a reason; tutoring cue: *"Tell me why you chose that — now write that on the drawing."*

## Challenge

Re-annotate one of your earlier sketches, replacing every weak annotation with a three-part one (feature + why + trade-off). Count how many marks you would now gain.

## Run sheet

0–10 preview (spec-linked annotation); 10–25 3D crating on project form; 25–35 annotation depth; 35–45 TA cue + challenge.

## Take-away

Bring your best-annotated sketch to the regular class as a model for peers.
