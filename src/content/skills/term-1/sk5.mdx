## Maps to

Regular Week 7 — Paper 4 Section A quick-fire 1: 12 topics, rapid-fire questions, mark-scheme precision. The exam session where precise technical vocabulary is the difference between 0 and 1.

## Preview

Section A format: 12 compulsory questions, breadth across the syllabus; command words (describe, explain, justify, calculate); the "no generic words" rule.

## Deepen

The key that unlocks Section A marks: precise technical vocabulary over vague claims. Reading past-paper mark schemes to internalise the expected language. How command words change the required response: "describe" lists features; "explain" needs a *because*; "justify" needs a reason *and* a comparison; "calculate" needs full working with units.

## Taste beyond

How mark schemes are constructed — indicative content vs credit-worthy alternatives; how A-level command-word hierarchy builds on IGCSE; why "strong" scores 0 but "high tensile strength due to its grain structure" scores 1.

## TA prep

Help peers identify exactly where their answers earn or lose marks; common error — descriptive answers to "explain" questions (no *because*); tutoring cue: *"What's missing from your answer that the mark scheme would expect?"*

## Challenge

Take five Section A questions across five different topics, answer them to top band, mark them to the scheme, then write a one-line tip for each that a peer could use.
