## Maps to

Regular Week 6 — drawing, building and testing the low-voltage switch-operated light; the current-limiting resistor.

## Preview

Circuit symbols; input–process–output; series vs parallel; switch types; the current-limiting resistor.

## Deepen

Ohm's law (V = IR); calculating the resistor from the supply and LED drop; series vs parallel behaviour; choosing the switch for the user.

## Taste beyond

Transistors as switches; sensors (LDR, thermistor); input–process–output *systems*; a glimpse of logic / programmable control.

## TA prep

Help peers wire and test safely; common errors — LED polarity, missing resistor, loose connections; tutoring cue: *"Trace the current from + all the way to − — where does it stop?"*

## Challenge

Design a sensor-triggered version of the light (e.g. an LDR night-light) as a stretch extension.

## Run sheet

0–10 preview; 10–26 Ohm's law & resistor calc; 26–36 sensors/systems taste; 36–45 TA rehearsal + challenge.

## Take-away

Calculate the resistor for a different LED and supply voltage.
