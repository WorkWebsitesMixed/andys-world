## Maps to

Regular Week 8 — research analysed into a justified, measurable specification (Criterion 2).

## Preview

Primary vs secondary research; ACCESS FM; what makes a spec point measurable and justified.

## Deepen

What lifts Criterion 2 to distinction — every point measurable, justified, traceable; reading the C2 mark scheme.

## Taste beyond

Engineering requirements and standards (BS/ISO); writing *testable* requirements; constraints vs criteria.

## TA prep

Help peers make spec points measurable; common error — vague "must be strong"; tutoring cue: *"How would you measure whether it passed?"*

## Challenge

Rewrite three weak spec points into measurable, justified ones with a test for each.

## Run sheet

0–10 preview; 10–25 distinction-level spec; 25–35 standards taste; 35–45 TA cue + challenge.

## Take-away

Draft your own product's specification to distinction standard.
