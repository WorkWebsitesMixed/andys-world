## Maps to

Regular Week 4 — woods and metals; properties and selection.

## Preview

Hardwood/softwood, ferrous/non-ferrous; working properties (strength, hardness, toughness, workability).

## Deepen

Precise meanings of tensile/compressive strength, stiffness, toughness, hardness; why mild steel vs aluminium for a given load; manufactured boards.

## Taste beyond

The stress–strain curve (elastic vs plastic, yield, ultimate strength) qualitatively; stiffness as Young's modulus; why metals deform (grain structure, dislocations) — a taste.

## TA prep

Help peers complete the properties table; common error — "strong" used generically; tutoring cue: *"Strong in what way — tension or compression?"*

## Challenge

Select materials for a riser carrying 5 kg and justify with properties *and* a sustainability trade-off.

## Run sheet

0–10 preview; 10–25 properties deep; 25–35 stress–strain taste; 35–45 TA cue + challenge.

## Take-away

Note one product where the "wrong" material was clearly chosen, and why.
