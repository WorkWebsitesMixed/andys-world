<Section title="What 'substantially complete' means" icon="✍️" id="topic">

By the end of this session, your report should have:

- **Introduction** — the question/problem and the specific fact that motivates it, written in full sentences.
- **Body section 1** — context or existing evidence, in full.
- **Body section 2** — your own analysis, in full.
- **Data/Evidence** — the table (and figure, if you have one) from B9, with a caption and referenced from the text using `\ref{}` — not just pasted in and left unmentioned.
- **Conclusion** — the claim, its evidence, and one honest limitation.
- **At least 3 citations**, spread across the sections that need them, not all bunched into one.

It does not need to be polished. A section with rough phrasing but the right content and a correct citation is further along than a beautifully worded paragraph with no evidence in it.

### Expanding a thin section

If a section is one sentence and you're stuck, ask: *what would someone need to know to believe this claim?* Answering that question in 2–3 more sentences is usually the missing content — not "adding more words," but adding the actual reasoning you skipped.

</Section>

<Activity title="Write time" time="35 min" grouping="Individual" materials={["Laptop", "Overleaf — your report project", "Your five content-plan sentences from B10", "Your sources"]}>
Work through your five sections in order. For each one:

1. Start from your B10 content-plan sentence.
2. Write it out in full — the claim, then the evidence that supports it, then (if relevant) a sentence connecting it to the next section.
3. Compile after each section, not just at the end — catch errors while you still remember what you just wrote.
4. If you finish early, go back and strengthen your weakest section — the one you flagged in B10's reflection.
</Activity>

<Support title="Stuck? Ask a specific question">
"I don't know what to do" doesn't get you unstuck quickly. Instead, name exactly what's broken:

- Not "my table doesn't work" → **"my table compiles but the caption isn't showing above it."**
- Not "my citation is wrong" → **"`\cite{}` shows a number but it's not the source I expected."**
- Not "I don't know what to write" → **"I have my claim for this section but no evidence for it yet — where do I look?"**

A specific question is one your teacher — or a classmate — can actually answer in one sentence.
</Support>
