This is the **final B session** — B sessions end here, before the real exam. There's no new topic today: this is a full drill across everything from B1–B6, timed to exam pace, followed by the personal revision list you'll actually use to study.

<Callout type="exam" title="Why this matters" id="why">
  A drill under time pressure reveals gaps that untimed review hides. If you can answer a question correctly with unlimited time but not in 30 seconds, that's still a gap — the real exam won't give you unlimited time either.
</Callout>

<Section title="Timed MCQ drill" icon="⏱️" id="drill">

Set a timer for **30 seconds per question** before you start — that's the real exam's pace. Don't stop to look anything up. Answer everything, mark it immediately after, and be completely honest with yourself about what you didn't know versus what you guessed correctly.
